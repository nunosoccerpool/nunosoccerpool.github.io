PRIZE DETAILS
                                   
Top 5 (Total Points)
Top 5 spots based on your combined Group and Knockout Stage points.
Tie-breaker: Most points in the Finals, then Semis, then entire Knockout Stage, then Group Stage.
                                   
"Half-Way" Prize
Awarded to the points leader after the Group Stage ends.
Tie-breaker: 1) Most groups with perfect 1st–4th order, 2) Most correct outcomes, 3) Most exact scores. 
If still tied, the prize is split.
                                   
"Bonus Questions" Side-Game
Most points from the 20 bonus questions.  Points do not count toward the main pool.
Tie-breaker: 1) Total number of correct answers, 2) Higher ranking in the main pool.
                                   
"Individual Group" Prizes
A small prize awarded to the sole leader in points (cannot be tied), in each of the 12 groups (A–L).
Tie-breaker:  Most points obtained in the Ranking portion of that respective group
                                   
"Refund" Prize
A random entry fee refund -- based on the Finals match. 
Criteria:  If the minute of the first goal or first card (whichever happens first) matches your ranking heading into the Final.
Example: The first card/goal is in the 14th minute and you were ranked 14th just before the Finals match, you win.
