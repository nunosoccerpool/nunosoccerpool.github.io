Prize Details

Official descriptions and tie-breaker criteria.

Top 5 (Main Standings)

Description: Top 5 spots based on your combined Group and Knockout Stage points.
Tie-breaker: Most points in the Finals, then Semis, then entire Knockout Stage, then Group Stage.

"Half-Way" Prize

Description: Awarded to the 1st place immediately following the conclusion of the Group Stage matches.
Tie-breaker: 1) Most groups with perfect 1st–4th order, 2) Most correct outcomes, 3) Most exact scores. If still tied, the prize is split evenly.

"Bonus Questions" Side-Game

Description: Most points from the 20 bonus questions side game. Note: These points do not count toward the main pool.
Tie-breaker: 1) Total number of correct answers, 2) Higher ranking in the main pool.

"Refund" Prize

Description: A random entry fee refund -- based on the Finals match.
Criteria: If the minute of the first goal or first card (whichever happens first) matches your ranking heading into the Final.
Example: The first card/goal is in the 14th minute and you were ranked 14th just before the Finals match, you win.

"Individual Group" Prizes (12)

Description: Independently awarded to the sole leader in points for each of the 12 groups (A–L).
Tie-breaker: Most points obtained in the Ranking portion of that respective group.
Sole Leader Rule: If two or more people are tied for the lead in a group, no prize is awarded for that group.
