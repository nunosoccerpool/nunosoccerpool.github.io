Points System

Point Allocation & Scoring System

Group Stage

Match Score Predictions

  • 1 pt Correctly predict the outcome of a match (Win, Loss, or Draw).
  • 1 pt Correctly predict the exact score of a match.

Group Rank Order

  • 4 pts Correctly predict the 1st place team in a group.
  • 5 pts Correctly predict the 1st place team + any other rank (e.g., 1st & 2nd, 1st & 3rd, or 1st & Last).
  • 9 pts Correctly predict the exact order of the group (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th).

  • 20 ptsper teamFor each team correctly predicted to reach the Quarters.
  • +8 ptscorrect scoreCorrectly predict exact score of a Quarter-finals match.

Semi-Finals

  • 40 ptsper teamFor each team correctly predicted to reach the Semis.
  • +16 ptscorrect scoreCorrectly predict exact score of a Semi-finals match.

Score requires both teams correct.

Finals / Champ

  • 80 ptsper teamFor each team correctly predicted to reach the Finals.
  • +32 ptscorrect scoreCorrectly predict exact score of a Finals match.
  • 120 ptsChampionsCorrectly predict the World Cup Champion.

Score requires both teams correct.

Consolation Match Note: Third place consolation match is not in scope for this pool. However, its total goals and cards accumulated still count toward applicable "Bonus Questions".
